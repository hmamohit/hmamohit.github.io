Hello! I'm H M A Mohit Chowdhury, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the <a href="https://www.unt.edu/" target="_blank">University of North Texas (UNT)</a>. I specialize in <u>machine learning</u>, <u>computer vision</u>, and <u>computational genomics</u>. As a Graduate Research Assistant at <a href="https://oluwadarelab.com/" target="_blank">Oluwadare Lab</a>, I am passionate about solving complex problems in <u>time-series forecasting</u>, <u>interpolation</u>, and <u>diffusion</u>. I focus particularly on applications in high-resolution genomic data.

My research aims to create new algorithms to study 3D genome organization using Hi-C data. I specialize in predicting chromatin loops and detecting Topologically Associating Domains (TADs). I apply <u>deep learning</u>, <u>computer vision</u>, <u>graph embeddings</u>, and <u>clustering</u> techniques to improve understanding in the biological field.

I am skilled in <u>Python</u>, <u>PyTorch</u>, <u>Java</u>, <u>Spring</u>, <u>C#</u>, <u>.NET</u> and cloud technologies like <u>AWS</u> and <u>Docker</u>. I have extensive experience building scalable systems and deploying machine learning models. I enjoy combining solid theoretical research with practical, real-world applications.

Outside of my work, I like to explore new places, capture moments through photography, spend quality time with friends, watch movies, and listen to music. My friends and family call me **Shadhin**, which means *"Independent"*. I try to embody this trait in my personal and professional life.

Feel free to explore my work and reach out if you’d like to collaborate!
